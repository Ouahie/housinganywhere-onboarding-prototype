Frequently asked questions

Know what happens before you start.

When can I review the booking terms?

You can complete your listing and review the applicable booking terms before you publish.

Do I lose control over tenant selection?

No. The proposed journey helps collect and structure information, but the landlord keeps the final acceptance decision.

Do I need to arrange physical viewings?

No. HousingAnywhere is designed around remote booking, so transparent property information, photos and videos support the tenant's decision.

When is the first rent paid out?

The first rental payment is held through the secure booking flow and is normally released to the landlord 48 hours after successful move-in.
